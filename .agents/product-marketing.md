# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v3
**Last updated:** 2026-08-20

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** A boutique couple-photography experience in Barcelona — styled, directed, and cinematic, built for real connection instead of posed tourist photos.
**What it does:** I Love You Barcelona is run by Nuria Alférez and Miguel Manzano — two photographer-artists (a couple) who direct and style intimate photo sessions across three "chapters" — *Love & The City*, *Wild At Heart*, and *In The Mood For Love* — designed to feel like a real moment, not a staged shot in front of a landmark.
**Product category:** Couple / romantic photography experience (Barcelona)
**Product type:** Local creative service — in-person session + edited digital gallery
**Business model:** Per-session pricing, three tiers (€400 / €550 / €650). No repeat-session guarantee offered. Future planned upsell: a personalized printed calendar, offered seasonally (e.g. Nov/Dec) to past clients — not live yet.
**Site:** www.iloveyoubarcelona.com (hosted on Squarespace) · Instagram (brand): @iloveyoutoobcn

## Target Audience
**Target customers:** Couples — both Barcelona locals and tourists/visitors who book in advance.
**Primary use case:** Wanting photos that actually look and feel like them — artistic, real chemistry — instead of a generic tourist photo at an iconic spot.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get a photography experience with real direction and styling, not a 10-minute snapshot
- Have something artistic and intimate to remember a trip, anniversary, or relationship milestone
- (Chapter 3) Create sensual, private photos as a couple — tasteful, never explicit
**Use cases:**
- Anniversary or honeymoon trip to Barcelona
- Engagement or "just because" romantic session
- Locals wanting a non-generic couple shoot
- Couples wanting a more intimate/sensual private session

## Personas
Not applicable — B2C, single decision-maker unit (the couple).

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Typical couple photographers in Barcelona shoot generic, posed photos at iconic tourist spots (Sagrada Família, Park Güell) that feel staged and impersonal.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- No creative direction or styling
- Cookie-cutter, overused locations
- Doesn't capture genuine connection — looks like every other tourist's photo
**What it costs them:** Money spent on photos they don't actually love or feel proud to share.
**Emotional tension:** Wanting to look and feel real together, not stiff and posed.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other Barcelona couple/proposal photographers (Flytographer-style, local wedding/couple photographers) — fall short because they're transactional, generic locations, no creative direction.
**Secondary:** Hiring a general/freelance photographer or asking a friend — falls short because there's no styling, no consistent artistic vision.
**Indirect:** Skipping professional photos, relying on phone/selfies — falls short because there's no artistic quality or professional edit.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Run by a real couple — Nuria Alférez and Miguel Manzano, two photographer-artists with their own fine-art/editorial portfolios (@nuria.alferez, @ojodearbol) — merging two visual styles into one brand
- Full creative direction + styling, cinematic mood (chapters named after films: *Love & The City*, *Wild At Heart*, *In The Mood For Love*)
- Deliberately non-iconic, personality-driven locations
- Three distinct experiences instead of one generic package — including a tasteful intimate/sensual tier, rare in this market
**How we do it differently:** Treat each session like directing a short film, not a photoshoot — mood, styling, and location chosen to build a story, not check off a landmark.
**Why that's better:** Photos with real emotional charge that couples actually want to share, not generic tourist documentation.
**Why customers choose us:** They've seen the style (via Instagram) and specifically want *that* — not a generic "Barcelona couple photographer."

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Is the intimate package explicit?" | No — sensual and tasteful, never pornographic; delivered privately, never shared without consent |
| "I'm not a model, will I feel awkward?" | Direction is built for real couples, not professional models — that awkwardness is exactly what the styling/direction removes |
| "What if it rains, or I'm only in town briefly?" | Flexible rescheduling; advance booking recommended for visitors |
| "Will it actually look like the portfolio?" | Same two photographers shoot every session — no bait-and-switch between marketing photos and delivered work |

**Anti-persona:** People who want a purely generic tourist/landmark photo with no direction or styling; anyone uncomfortable with sensual content should self-select out of Chapter 3 via its description.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Existing photographers feel impersonal, touristy, transactional.
**Pull:** Cinematic, personal, artistic style; real couple behind the lens; visible portfolio proof on Instagram.
**Habit:** Defaulting to the first "Barcelona couple photographer" Google result, or skipping professional photos entirely.
**Anxiety:** Booking a stranger for something intimate; justifying the price; trusting the portfolio reflects what they'll actually get.

## Goals
**Business goal:** Move from portfolio-building/pre-launch to first paying bookings; establish local reputation in Barcelona.
**Conversion action:** Submit inquiry via contact form (no booking calendar yet — planned for later).
**Current metrics:** None — pre-launch.
**Capacity:** 1–3 sessions/month today (Tuesdays only), scalable to 4–8/month later. Current goal is supplemental income, not full-time. Scarcity ("only 1–3 couples a month") is treated as an honest positioning point, not manufactured urgency.
**Distribution strategy:** Own site is the primary, full-brand, full-price channel (all three chapters). Google Search Console + sitemap submission still needed before any SEO/content work can pay off.
