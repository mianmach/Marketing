ACT I

The Story

You're not a photo studio. You're a couple, photographing couples — which is why the work doesn't look staged. Two working artists, Nuria Alférez and Miguel Manzano, merged their personal visual languages into one brand, built around direction and styling instead of a snapshot in front of a landmark.

That's the whole positioning: Barcelona has no shortage of couple photographers. It has almost none who direct, style, and treat a 90-minute session like a short film.
